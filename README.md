# How Your Body Handles Glucose

An interactive, animated explainer of how the human body manages blood sugar: the pancreas, insulin and glucagon, the liver, muscle, fat, the brain, exercise, low and high blood sugar, type 1 and type 2 diabetes, muscle mass, protein, and balanced meals.

**Live page:** https://akashsaini21.github.io/glucose-guide/

**Concept by Akash Saini · Built with Claude**

## About the content

- Every factual claim on the page was checked against guideline bodies (ADA, CDC, NIH, Endocrine Society), peer-reviewed research or standard medical textbooks. The full source list is at the bottom of the page.
- Numbers and hormone levels on the live map are illustrative examples, not measurements.
- This is an educational overview, not medical advice. Speak to a doctor about your own blood sugar.
